# Surface: Counter Books dashboard

Visitor mode: Operate

Audience: the shop owner, alone at the counter during and after trade.
Job: read profit, cash, spend, and stock in one glance, then post a sale or expense without leaving the board.
Proof: live PKR figures, a sales-versus-expenses pair, inventory plates, labeled sample books.
Constraints: local-only browser data, PKR, retail computer catalog, no accounts.

Memorable moment: four backlit fascia modules answer the day; posting a sale settles cash and profit like channel letters changing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The books are a night shop fascia, not a SaaS card grid. Four acrylic modules answer the day; sales and expenses stay visible as opposing members.

OWN-WORLD: Night shutter #0e1418, ice acrylic #d7e6ea, shop cyan #1aa3c4, tungsten spill #c45c2a, alert red #e24b4b. Extruded condensed caps, LED module pitch, aluminum seams, packing tags on live stock. No card chrome.

STORY: The owner reads four lit totals, sees sales pulling against expenses, posts the next till event, and notices low stock by a tag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width aluminum name bar. Four equal fascia modules. Cyan ticker. Left: sales versus expenses columns. Right: till keys RECORD SALE and RECORD EXPENSE. Bottom: inventory bin plates. Primary action sits in the till keys.

FORM: Night Fascia, grounded candidate 7 of 7, seed key b3971cf5. Signature interaction: the ticker plus module figures settling on each posting; flash reserved for alerts.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
